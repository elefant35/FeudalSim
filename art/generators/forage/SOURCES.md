# P1 botanical art references

These sources inform original procedural morphology. No source images, models or
textures were downloaded or copied. Species names are the repo node IDs; the
generator's simplified geometry is for the game, not a botanical field guide.

The remaining herbs follow conventional species silhouettes: basal plantain
rosettes and seed spikes; flat yarrow heads; daisy-like feverfew; airy meadowsweet
panicles; low thyme; sphagnum cushions; valerian flower heads; mint's paired leaves
and flower whorls; serrated nettles; arrow-leaved sorrel; loose oat panicles;
broad sea-beet and cabbage rosettes; pea vines/pods; small blue flax flowers; hop
vines/cones; and trifoliate strawberry foliage with small red fruit. Any unresolved
morphology is recorded in the generator README as an art simplification.

The shared palette has no purple/pink botanical swatch. Existing `woad`, `madder`
and pale skin/linen tones approximate the hue in different faces without adding
textures. Hemlock blotches are therefore muted blue, with the distinctive spotted
pattern intact. Foliage stays in the foliage row, so seasonal shaders can shift it.
Flower/fruit nodes are separate from leaves when seasonal hiding is useful.

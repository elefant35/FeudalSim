Verified in Godot 4.7.2 by `art/generators/animations/godot_forward_probe.gd`: eyes/toes are forward +Z, planted walk foot travels −Z at 1.600015 m/s, cancelling actor motion +Z at 1.6 m/s. The asset and gait directions agree. Keep these integration changes in the code-owned files; do not rotate every exported asset against the contract.
